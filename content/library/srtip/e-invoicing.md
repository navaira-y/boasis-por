# E-invoicing

When the federal e-invoicing programme reaches your size.

Applies to: revenue over a threshold

## What it is

The Ministry of Finance e-invoicing programme, delivered through accredited service providers (52 accredited, 8 pre-approved at the time of the research). Free zone specifics are not in the research.

## When it applies

Companies with revenue of AED 50m or more in the first wave (reported).

## When it is due

Go-live 1 January 2027 for revenue of AED 50m or more; appoint an accredited service provider by 30 October 2026 (reported).

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no penalty.

## The steps

1. Accountant: check revenue against AED 50m.
2. Accountant: choose an accredited service provider from the Ministry of Finance list by 30 October 2026.
3. Accountant: go live by 1 January 2027.

## Documents needed

- Revenue figures
- The provider agreement
