# Annual licence renewal

The SRTIP renewal: the research has no zone-specific process yet.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

Renewing the SRTIP licence. The research gives a general free zone pattern only: the licence, the lease and often the establishment card renew as one yearly package (confirmed for DMCC, not for SRTIP).

## When it applies

Every licensee, at every licence expiry.

## When it is due

Unknown. The SRTIP term, lead time and earliest window are not in the research.

## What it costs

Unknown. The package prices (AED 3,999, 6,499, 8,499) are confirmed, but the research does not say they are renewal fees.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no SRTIP late-renewal rule.

## The steps

Unknown. The zone-specific checklist is not in the research. The playbook in the authority file is empty until it is.

## Documents needed

Unknown.

## Matching card

`licence-renewal`
