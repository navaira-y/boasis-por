# Health insurance

The Northern Emirates basic scheme: who must be covered, and who pays.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas

## What it is

Sharjah is under the Northern Emirates basic health insurance scheme from 1 January 2025: the employer pays for a basic policy when any residence permit is issued or renewed (confirmed). Workers bear no cost. The employer is not obliged to cover dependants; the sponsor may buy the AED 320 product for them (confirmed).

## When it applies

From the first person you sponsor. Permits issued before 2025 join at their next renewal.

## When it is due

Before the residence application; the zone form requires proof. The policy runs 2 years (confirmed).

## What it costs

AED 320 a year for the basic policy (confirmed).

## What happens if you miss it

No residence is issued or renewed without the policy (confirmed). The research gives no fine for Sharjah.

## The steps

1. Owner: buy the basic policy for each employee before the residence application.
2. PRO: produce it at residence issuance and renewal through the zone.
3. Owner: renew the policy before it ends, ahead of each visa renewal.

## Documents needed

- The insurance policy per person
