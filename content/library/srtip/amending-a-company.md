# Amending a company

Activities, manager, shareholders, address, name and quota at SRTIP: not yet in the research.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

A change to the SRTIP licence. The research gives the free zone pattern only: a resolution plus the zone form, a power of attorney if an attorney signs, and the zone attests without an external notary in most zones (confirmed for RAKEZ, not SRTIP).

## When it applies

Whenever activities, the manager, the partners, the address, the name or the visa package change.

## When it is due

Before you act on the change. An ownership change must reach the beneficial owner register within 15 days (confirmed, federal).

## What it costs

Unknown. Moving up a package: STARTER AED 3,999, GROWTH 6,499, ELITE 8,499 (confirmed prices; the upgrade rule is not stated).

## What happens if you miss it

An ownership change not reflected in the beneficial owner register within 15 days draws a warning, then AED 15,000, then AED 30,000 (confirmed).

## The steps

Unknown for the zone. The federal step that is known: Owner: update the beneficial owner declaration within 15 days of an ownership change.

## Documents needed

Unknown.

## Matching card

`licence-amendment`
