# Working with a PRO

What a PRO can do at SRTIP, and what stays with you.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

At SRTIP the Investor Services Department is the only authorised channel for immigration applications; the PRO is named on the zone form and declarations are signed before a zone officer (confirmed). A PRO is a convenience, not a legal requirement (reported).

## When it applies

Whenever you delegate typing, submission, appointments or collection.

## When it is due

No date.

## What it costs

Retainers of AED 1,000 to 2,000 a month up to 10 staff, 2,500 to 5,000 for 10 to 50, or AED 500 to 1,500 per visa; government fees passed through (reported, market-wide).

## What happens if you miss it

Sharing your UAE PASS is prohibited by its terms, and sharing credentials exposes you under Decree-Law 34/2021 (confirmed terms).

## The steps

1. Owner: name the PRO on the zone form.
2. Owner: give the PRO the licence, the establishment card and the signatory passports.
3. PRO: file at the Investor Services Department and log every reference number in the portal.
4. Owner: keep documents, payroll, insurance and the decisions.

## Documents needed

- Licence and establishment card
- Signatory passports
- The zone form naming the PRO

## Matching card

`working-with-a-pro` (no compliance card in spec section 11 covers this topic; the id is the library entry id)
