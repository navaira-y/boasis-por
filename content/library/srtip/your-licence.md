# Your licence

What your SRTIP licence package allows, and what the research does not say.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

An SRTIP (SPark) licence is sold as a package: SPARK STARTER with 0 visas, GROWTH with 1 visa, ELITE with 2 visas (confirmed). The activities it allows, and the consequence of operating outside them, are not in the research: unknown.

## When it applies

From the day the licence is issued until it is cancelled.

## When it is due

The licence term is not in the research: unknown.

## What it costs

STARTER AED 3,999; GROWTH AED 6,499; ELITE AED 8,499 (confirmed). Whether these are yearly is not stated.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no penalty for operating outside the licence at SRTIP.

## The steps

1. Owner: keep a copy of the licence and its package in the portal.
2. Owner: ask the zone before adding an activity or a visa beyond the package.

## Documents needed

- The licence
