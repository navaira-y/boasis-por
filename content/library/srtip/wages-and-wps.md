# Wages and WPS

Whether WPS reaches SRTIP is not published; here is what is known.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas

## What it is

The Wage Protection System is mandated for MOHRE-licensed establishments under Ministerial Resolution 340/2026. SRTIP publishes nothing on WPS (not published). MOHRE guidance has no free-zone wording, and secondary sources conflict on which zones are inside (unclear).

## When it applies

Unclear for SRTIP. Federal labour law still applies to the wage itself.

## When it is due

If WPS applies: the 1st of each month for the preceding month from 1 June 2026 (confirmed for MOHRE establishments). The SRTIP rule: unknown.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown for SRTIP.

## The steps

1. Owner: ask the zone in writing whether WPS applies to your licence, and keep the answer in the portal.
2. Accountant: pay wages in AED by the pay period end and keep evidence of every payment.

## Documents needed

- Payroll records and payment evidence
