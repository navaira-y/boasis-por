# Your office

Packages, the visa allowance per package, and what is not in the research.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

SRTIP sells packages rather than square metres: STARTER 0 visas, GROWTH 1 visa, ELITE 2 visas (confirmed). Ejari is the Dubai register; Sharjah uses the municipality lease (reported), so Ejari does not apply here.

## When it applies

Every licensee.

## When it is due

Unknown. Whether the office term follows the licence, and rent, fit-out and hand-back rules, are not in the research.

## What it costs

STARTER AED 3,999; GROWTH AED 6,499; ELITE AED 8,499 (confirmed).

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown.

## The steps

1. Owner: choose the package that matches the number of visas you need.
2. Owner: ask the zone before adding visas beyond the package.

## Documents needed

- The package agreement

## Matching card

`office-lease-and-ejari`
