# Employment and labour law

Federal law applies at SRTIP: contracts, probation, notice, leave and end of service.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas

## What it is

SRTIP publishes no employment rules of its own; Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 and Cabinet Resolution 1/2022 apply (confirmed). The contract states the parties, job, start date, workplace, hours, rest days, probation, term, wage with allowances, leave, notice and termination procedure (confirmed, Art 10). Contracts are fixed-term (reported).

## When it applies

Every employee you sponsor through the zone.

## When it is due

Probation at most 6 months (confirmed). Gratuity within 14 days of leaving (reported); permitted deductions within 3 months (confirmed). Whether a contract is registered with the zone, and by when, is not in the research: unknown.

## What it costs

Unknown. Gratuity: 21 days of basic wage per year for the first 5 years, 30 days after, capped at 2 years, after 1 year of service (reported).

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no penalty for SRTIP.

## The steps

1. Owner: sign a contract that carries the Art 10 content.
2. Owner: decide before the probation end; 14 days notice from the employer during probation, 30 to 90 days after (reported).
3. Accountant: at leaving, pay gratuity within 14 days and deductions within 3 months.
4. PRO: cancel the residence through the zone.

## Documents needed

- Signed employment contract
- Leave and wage records
