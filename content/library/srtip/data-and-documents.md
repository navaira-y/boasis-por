# Data and documents

What you may keep, Emirates ID copies and storing employee data.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

The Personal Data Protection Law (Decree-Law 45/2021) applies to employee data; the health data law (Federal Law 2/2019) reaches free zones and bars storing health data outside the UAE without approval (reported). A business may take the data on an Emirates ID but may not withhold the card (confirmed). No rule against retaining copies was found.

## When it applies

Every licensee holding employee data.

## When it is due

No date. Whether an employer-held insurance certificate or medical result counts as health data is unclear, so the portal keeps medical reports out of the vault.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Health data stored or transferred outside the UAE without approval carries fines of AED 500,000 to 700,000 (reported).

## The steps

1. Owner: keep employee files in the UAE.
2. Owner: read the Emirates ID for its data; never hold the card.
3. Owner: keep only metadata about medical results, not the reports.
4. Owner: never share UAE PASS or portal credentials with anyone.

## Documents needed

- None beyond your own records
