# Cancelling or closing a company

The general free zone closing pattern, and what is not yet known for SRTIP.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

The research gives the free zone pattern: a termination application, visas cancelled, no-objection certificates, a liquidator or auditor where the zone requires one, then deregistration (reported, not SRTIP-specific). The SRTIP window and its fees inside and outside it are not in the research.

## When it applies

When you stop trading, or decide not to renew.

## When it is due

Unknown for the zone. Notice periods at other zones run 14 to 45 days (reported). VAT deregistration within 20 business days (reported); corporate tax deregistration within 3 months of cessation (reported). The portal asks you 90 days before expiry whether you renew, cancel or shrink.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no SRTIP rule.

## The steps

1. Owner: file the termination application with the zone.
2. PRO: cancel every visa through the zone Investor Services Department, dependants first (or the AED 5,000 guarantee), after settling entitlements.
3. Owner: collect the no-objection certificates the zone asks for.
4. Owner: appoint a liquidator or auditor where the zone requires one.
5. Accountant: apply for VAT deregistration within 20 business days and corporate tax deregistration within 3 months.
6. Zone: deregister the company and issue the cancellation.

## Documents needed

- Termination application
- Visa cancellation confirmations
- No-objection certificates
- Tax deregistration confirmations
