# UBO, who owns your company

The beneficial owner declaration and register under Cabinet Decision 109/2023.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

A declaration of the natural persons who ultimately own or control the company, and a register kept up to date. The rule is federal. Where an SRTIP company files is not in the research.

## When it applies

Every licensee.

## When it is due

Within 60 days of licensing; updates within 15 days of any change (confirmed). Claims of 2025 and 2026 amendments are unverified.

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no fee.

## What happens if you miss it

A register not updated draws a warning, then AED 15,000, then AED 30,000; a register not kept, a warning, then AED 50,000, then AED 100,000; licence suspension on a third breach (confirmed, CD 132/2023).

## The steps

1. Owner: identify every beneficial owner.
2. Owner or PRO: file the declaration within 60 days of licensing.
3. Owner: keep the register and file each change within 15 days.
4. Owner: confirm no change at each renewal.

## Documents needed

- The ownership chain
- IDs of the beneficial owners
