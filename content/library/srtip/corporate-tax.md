# Corporate tax

Registration, Small Business Relief, the return and the free zone regime.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

Federal corporate tax administered by the FTA through EmaraTax. Free zone companies register like everyone else (confirmed). A qualifying free zone person claiming the free zone regime needs audited statements (confirmed).

## When it applies

Every licensee. Natural persons with business turnover above AED 1m also register.

## When it is due

Registration within 3 months of incorporation, free zones included (confirmed, FTA Decision 3/2024). The return and payment 9 months after the end of the tax period (confirmed). Deregistration within 3 months of cessation (reported).

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no rate. Small Business Relief up to AED 3m revenue; cash basis up to AED 3m; audited statements above AED 50m revenue or when claiming the free zone regime (confirmed).

## What happens if you miss it

AED 10,000 for late registration (confirmed), waived if the first return is filed within 7 months of the first period end.

## The steps

1. Accountant: register on EmaraTax within 3 months of incorporation.
2. Owner: choose the financial year end; it sets the return date.
3. Accountant: decide each period between Small Business Relief and the free zone regime; the regime needs an audit.
4. Accountant: file the return and pay within 9 months of the period end.
5. Accountant: deregister within 3 months of cessation.

## Documents needed

- Licence and memorandum
- Financial statements (audited for the free zone regime or above AED 50m)
- Related-party schedule above AED 40m (confirmed)
