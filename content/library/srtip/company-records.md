# Company records

General assembly, registers and resolutions.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

The records a company keeps: the beneficial owner register, resolutions and general assembly minutes. The general assembly rule for an SRTIP company is not in the research: unknown.

## When it applies

Every licensee.

## When it is due

Unknown for the general assembly. The beneficial owner register is updated within 15 days of a change (confirmed).

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

A beneficial owner register not maintained draws a warning, then AED 50,000, then AED 100,000 (confirmed).

## The steps

1. Owner: keep the beneficial owner register current.
2. Owner: keep resolutions and zone forms with the licence file.

## Documents needed

- Beneficial owner register
- Resolutions

## Matching card

`general-assembly`
