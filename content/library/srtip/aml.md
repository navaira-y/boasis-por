# AML, does it apply to you

Registration on goAML for the listed activities.

Applies to: activity listed

## What it is

Designated non-financial businesses register with the Ministry of Economy on SACM and then goAML (confirmed, federal). The listed activities in the research: real estate, auditors and accountants, precious metals, trust and company service providers.

## When it applies

Only when your licence carries one of the listed activities. Zone specifics are not in the research.

## When it is due

Unknown. The research gives no deadline.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no penalty.

## The steps

1. Owner: check whether an activity on the licence is in the list.
2. Owner or accountant: register on SACM, then on goAML.
3. Owner: re-check after every activity amendment.

## Documents needed

- The licence
