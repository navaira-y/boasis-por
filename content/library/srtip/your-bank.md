# Your bank

KYC, signatories and what banks ask for.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

The company account. The research gives no SRTIP-specific bank facts. Generally: banks restrict accounts when the licence on file has expired (reported); the bank is one of the clearances at closing (reported). KYC refresh and signatory rules are not in the research: unknown.

## When it applies

Every licensee.

## When it is due

Unknown. Send the renewed or amended licence to the bank after each change.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

An expired licence on file brings bank restrictions (reported).

## The steps

1. Owner: send the renewed licence to every bank after each renewal and amendment.
2. Bank: clear and close the account at company closure.

## Documents needed

- Current licence
- Signatory letters

`bank-account`
