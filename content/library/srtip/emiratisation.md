# Emiratisation

The research covers Emiratisation for mainland companies only.

Applies to: headcount over a threshold

## What it is

MOHRE targets for hiring Emiratis apply to MOHRE-licensed companies with 50 or more staff, and 20 to 49 staff in 14 sectors (confirmed for mainland). Whether they reach SRTIP licensees is not in the research: unknown.

## When it applies

Unknown for SRTIP.

## When it is due

Unknown for SRTIP.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown for SRTIP.

## The steps

1. Owner: if your headcount passes 20, ask the zone whether the targets apply and keep the answer in the portal.
