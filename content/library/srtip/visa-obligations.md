# Visa obligations end to end

The SRTIP visa chain through the Investor Services Department, and what you must do as a sponsor.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas

## What it is

The zone sponsors your people, not MOHRE (confirmed, federal). Applications go only through the SRTIP Investor Services Department, counter-based, with a PRO named on the form and declarations signed before a zone officer (confirmed). Residence permits are issued by ICP.

## When it applies

Every person you sponsor, within the package allowance (STARTER 0, GROWTH 1, ELITE 2).

## When it is due

The entry permit runs 60 days; residence must be completed within 55 days (confirmed). Renew before the last 20 days of the visa (confirmed). Passport at least 8 months for a new visa, 6 at renewal (confirmed). Visa validity 2 years (reported). Residence lapses after more than 180 consecutive days abroad (confirmed, federal).

## What it costs

Zone fees not published; reported at about AED 2,233 to 3,510 all-in. ICP residence AED 100 application plus 100 a year plus 100 smart service; status adjustment AED 500 (confirmed). Medical at EHS, Category A employees AED 250 (confirmed). Basic insurance AED 320 a year (confirmed).

## What happens if you miss it

Residency not completed within the ICP 60 days carries AED 50 a day (confirmed, ICP); the SRTIP 55-day rule sits inside it.

## The steps

1. Owner: check the package allows another visa.
2. Owner or PRO: file at the Investor Services Department; the PRO is named on the form.
3. Zone: obtains the entry permit, valid 60 days.
4. Person: enter or change status; residence within 55 days.
5. Owner: buy the Northern Emirates basic health insurance before the residence.
6. Person, paid by the owner: medical fitness test at an EHS centre; the form requires the report.
7. Zone: residence permit and Emirates ID through ICP.

## Documents needed

- Passport with at least 8 months left (6 at renewal)
- Photo
- The zone form with the PRO named
- Insurance policy and medical report at the residence step
