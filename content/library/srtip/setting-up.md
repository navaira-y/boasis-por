# Setting up

The day-0 list after an SRTIP licence is issued.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

The registrations that follow the licence: the immigration establishment card through the zone desk, bank, tax, beneficial owner, and, before the first visa, the basic health insurance.

## When it applies

Every new licensee, and any existing company that skipped an item.

## When it is due

Corporate tax registration within 3 months of incorporation, free zones included (confirmed). Beneficial owner declaration within 60 days (confirmed). The immigration card comes before any visa. Insurance before the first residence.

## What it costs

ICP establishment card AED 100 plus 100 plus 1,000 subscription plus 100, yearly (confirmed, federal ICP page). Basic insurance AED 320 a year per person (confirmed). Zone establishment card fees: not published.

## What happens if you miss it

AED 10,000 for late corporate tax registration (confirmed); a missing beneficial owner declaration draws a warning, then AED 15,000 (confirmed).

## The steps

1. Owner or PRO: apply for the immigration establishment card through the zone Investor Services Department.
2. Owner: open the bank account.
3. Accountant: register for corporate tax within 3 months.
4. Owner or PRO: file the beneficial owner declaration within 60 days.
5. Accountant: start the quarterly VAT threshold watch.
6. Owner: buy the basic health insurance before the first residence application.
7. Owner: ask the zone in writing whether WPS applies and keep the answer.
8. Owner: register on goAML if an activity is listed.

## Documents needed

- Licence
- Owners passports and IDs

## Matching card

`setting-up` (no single compliance card in spec section 11 covers this list; the id is the library entry id)
