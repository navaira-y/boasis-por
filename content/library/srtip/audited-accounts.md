# Audited accounts and record keeping

When an audit is required, and what the research does not say for SRTIP.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

Audited financial statements. The research confirms one trigger: corporate tax requires audited statements above AED 50m revenue or when the company claims the free zone regime. Companies up to AED 3m revenue may use cash basis accounting (confirmed).

## When it applies

Whether SRTIP requires an audit for licence renewal is not in the research: unknown.

## When it is due

Unknown for renewal. For corporate tax, with the return, 9 months after the period end.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no penalty.

## The steps

1. Owner: choose the financial year end at setup.
2. Accountant: keep the books on IFRS, or cash basis up to AED 3m revenue.
3. Accountant: commission the audit when claiming the free zone regime or above AED 50m revenue.

## Documents needed

- Financial statements
- The audit report where required
