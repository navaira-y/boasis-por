# Working with a PRO

What a PRO can do, and what stays with you.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

A PRO is a person or firm that handles MOHRE, DET, GDRFA and ICP submissions. Using one is a convenience, not a legal requirement (reported). A PRO firm needs the Documents Clearing Services activity, which covers submission, not advice (reported).

## When it applies

Whenever you delegate typing, submission, appointments or collection.

## When it is due

No date.

## What it costs

Retainers of AED 1,000 to 2,000 a month up to 10 staff, 2,500 to 5,000 for 10 to 50, or AED 500 to 1,500 per visa; government fees passed through (reported).

## What happens if you miss it

Sharing your UAE PASS is prohibited by its terms, and sharing credentials exposes you under Decree-Law 34/2021 (confirmed terms).

## The steps

1. Owner: add the PRO by Emirates ID under Manage Users on the government portal (MoEI pattern, confirmed).
2. Owner: give the PRO the licence, immigration and MOHRE cards, signatory passports and a notarised authorisation.
3. PRO: obtain an e-signature card for MOHRE and, where needed, a GDRFA PRO card.
4. Owner: keep documents, payroll, insurance and the decisions.
5. PRO: log every reference number in the portal.

## Documents needed

- Licence, immigration card, MOHRE card
- Signatory passports, partners appendix
- Notarised authorisation in Arabic
