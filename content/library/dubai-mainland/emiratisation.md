# Emiratisation

Targets for mainland companies with 50 or more staff, and for 20 to 49 staff in 14 sectors.

Applies to: mainland; headcount over a threshold

## What it is

MOHRE targets for hiring Emiratis in skilled jobs. Companies with 50 or more employees add 2 percentage points a year, reaching 10 percent by end 2026 (confirmed). Companies with 20 to 49 employees in the 14 listed sectors hired one Emirati in 2024 and two in 2025 (confirmed).

## When it applies

Mainland companies at those headcounts. The research does not cover free zones.

## When it is due

1 percent by 30 June and 1 percent by 31 December for the 50-plus group (the half-year split is reported, the yearly 2 percent is confirmed).

## What it costs

Unknown. The hiring cost is yours; the contribution for a missing hire is in the line below.

## What happens if you miss it

A contribution of AED 10,000 a month per missing Emirati in 2026 for the 50-plus group (confirmed by schedule); AED 96,000 or 108,000 per missing hire for the 20 to 49 group (confirmed).

## The steps

1. Owner: count your staff and check the sector list.
2. Owner: plan the hires before 30 June and 31 December.
3. Owner: register the hires with MOHRE.

## Documents needed

- Headcount by skill level
- MOHRE registration of each Emirati hire
