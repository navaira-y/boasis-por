# Amending a company

Activities, manager, shareholders, address, name and visa quota: what each change takes.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

A change to the DET licence. The rule of thumb in the research: anything that touches the memorandum of association needs a notary, in person or by remote video with UAE PASS. Activity adds, manager swaps and address changes that do not touch the memorandum are online.

## When it applies

Whenever activities, the manager, the partners, the address, the trade name or the legal form change. A larger visa quota is a MOHRE matter, driven by the lease size.

## When it is due

Before you act on the change. An ownership change must reach the beneficial owner register within 15 days (Cabinet Decision 109/2023).

## What it costs

Activity change from about AED 500 (reported). Trade name about AED 2,000 to 6,000 (reported). Share transfer about AED 1,500 to 5,000 government plus 0.25 percent notary (reported). Manager, address, legal form and branch: unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

An ownership change not reflected in the beneficial owner register within 15 days draws a warning, then AED 15,000, then AED 30,000 (confirmed).

## The steps

1. Owner: pass the partner resolution. A manager change needs the resolution, the IDs and a no-objection letter.
2. Owner or PRO: obtain a no-objection certificate from the regulator for a regulated activity.
3. Owner: notarise a memorandum addendum when the objects, name, partners or legal form change.
4. Owner or PRO: for a share transfer, get DET initial approval, then the notarised transfer, then submit for reissue.
5. Owner or PRO: submit the amendment online at DET.
6. Owner: for an address change, register the new Ejari first.
7. Owner or PRO: for more visas, ask MOHRE for a recalculation after a larger lease, or an exceptional quota with contracts (about 2 working days, reported).
8. Owner: update the beneficial owner declaration within 15 days if ownership changed.
9. Owner: upload the amended licence to the portal.

## Documents needed

- Partner resolution
- IDs of the people involved
- No-objection certificates where needed
- Notarised memorandum addendum where the memorandum changes
- New Ejari for an address change
