# Annual licence renewal

What must be ready before the DET renewal date, and the order to do it in.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

Renewing the DET licence, then the Dubai Chamber membership. The renewal is submitted on Invest in Dubai (UAE PASS), DET eServices, SMS 6969 or Dubai Now, by the owner or a delegated PRO.

## When it applies

Every licensee, at every licence expiry.

## When it is due

At the licence expiry date. Submitting at least 30 days early is recommended (reported). The earliest window is not published. The licence term itself is not stated in the research.

## What it costs

Licence, market and chamber fees typically AED 8,000 to 15,000 (reported). Chamber renewal AED 50 to 2,200 (reported). The Ejari renewal carries a DLD fee, amount not in the research.

## What happens if you miss it

A reported AED 250 a month, plus 10 percent after about 60 days; MOHRE freezes permits, GDRFA blocks renewals and banks restrict accounts (reported, no DET page found).

## The steps

1. Owner or PRO: renew the Ejari so the tenancy has at least one month left (three or more recommended).
2. Owner or PRO: clear every pending DET fine.
3. Owner: gather the Ejari, the memorandum of association, the partner IDs and any external approvals.
4. Owner or PRO: submit the renewal on Invest in Dubai, DET eServices, SMS 6969 or Dubai Now.
5. Owner: pay the licence, market and chamber fees.
6. Owner or PRO: renew the Dubai Chamber membership after the licence.
7. Owner: upload the renewed licence to the portal.

## Documents needed

- Valid Ejari
- Memorandum of association
- Partner IDs
- External approvals where the activity needs them
