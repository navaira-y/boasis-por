# Your office

Leases, Ejari, the visa quota by size, moving and handing back.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

A Dubai mainland company needs a tenancy registered in Ejari. The Ejari feeds the licence renewal and the MOHRE visa quota. A flexi-desk is not accepted for the quota (reported).

## When it applies

Every licensee. The quota part applies once you sponsor visas.

## When it is due

At licence renewal the Ejari must have at least one month left; three or more is recommended (reported). Rent instalment dates, fit-out approvals and rent rules are not in the research.

## What it costs

Unknown. The Ejari carries a DLD fee, amount not in the research.

## What happens if you miss it

An Ejari with under a month left gets the licence renewal rejected (reported).

## The steps

1. Owner: sign the tenancy and register it in Ejari (DLD Ejari, a typing centre or Dubai REST).
2. Owner or PRO: renew the Ejari before the licence renewal.
3. Owner: for more visas, take a larger lease; the quota is roughly one visa per 9 square metres, some sources say 10 to 12, and no emirate publishes a ratio (reported, low confidence).
4. Owner: when moving, register the new Ejari, then amend the licence address online.
5. Owner: for a branch, a separate Ejari and DET approval, about 3 to 4 weeks (thin source).
6. Owner: when handing back, clear DEWA, telecom and the landlord.

## Documents needed

- Signed tenancy contract
- Ejari certificate
