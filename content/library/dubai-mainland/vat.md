# VAT

Thresholds, returns and deregistration.

Applies to: all licensees (the threshold watch); VAT registered (the returns)

## What it is

Value added tax administered by the FTA on EmaraTax. Registration is mandatory above AED 375,000 taxable turnover and voluntary above AED 187,500 (confirmed). The return (VAT201) needs standard-rated sales by emirate, reverse charge, zero-rated, exempt, imports, adjustments and input tax (confirmed).

## When it applies

Every licensee watches the threshold. Registered companies file returns.

## When it is due

Apply within 30 days of crossing the threshold (reported). The return deadline is not in the research. Deregister within 20 business days of no longer being required to register (reported).

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no fee.

## What happens if you miss it

AED 10,000 for late registration (reported); late deregistration AED 1,000, then 1,000 a month, capped at 10,000 (reported).

## The steps

1. Accountant: answer the turnover question every quarter.
2. Accountant: register on EmaraTax within 30 days of crossing AED 375,000.
3. Accountant: file each return with the VAT201 boxes and pay.
4. Accountant: deregister within 20 business days when no longer required.

## Documents needed

- Turnover records by emirate and tax code
- Customs records for imports
