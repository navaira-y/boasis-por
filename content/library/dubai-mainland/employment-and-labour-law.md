# Employment and labour law

Contracts, probation, notice, leave and end of service under Federal Decree-Law 33/2021.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas

## What it is

Federal Decree-Law 33/2021 and Cabinet Resolution 1/2022 govern every mainland employment. All contracts are fixed-term (reported). The contract must state the parties, job, start date, workplace, hours, rest days, probation, term, wage with allowances, leave, notice and termination procedure (confirmed, Art 10).

## When it applies

Every employee on a MOHRE work permit. Freelancers under Art 8 are not your workers.

## When it is due

The labour contract is registered with MOHRE within 14 days of entry, status change or permit issuance; sources differ on the trigger (reported). Probation is at most 6 months (confirmed). Gratuity is paid within 14 days of leaving (reported); permitted deductions within 3 months (confirmed, Art 29).

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no fee for contract registration. Gratuity: 21 days of basic wage per year for the first 5 years, 30 days after, capped at 2 years of wage, after 1 year of service (reported).

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no penalty for a late contract registration.

## The steps

1. Owner: e-sign the MOHRE job offer with the person before the permit application.
2. PRO: register the labour contract at MOHRE within 14 days.
3. Owner: decide before the probation end; the employer gives 14 days notice during probation, 30 to 90 days after (reported).
4. Accountant: at leaving, pay final wages through WPS by the 1st, gratuity within 14 days, deductions within 3 months.
5. PRO: cancel the permit; the establishment acknowledges all entitlements are paid (confirmed, Art 7(3)).
6. Owner: an absence over 7 days with no contact is reported to MOHRE on its form (confirmed, Art 28).

## Documents needed

- Signed MOHRE job offer
- Registered labour contract
- Leave and wage records
