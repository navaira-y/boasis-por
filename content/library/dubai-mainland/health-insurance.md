# Health insurance

Who must be covered in Dubai, and who pays.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas

## What it is

Dubai Law 11/2013 makes the employer enrol every employee, bear the cost and not charge it back (Art 9 to 10). The policy is produced when a residence is issued or renewed (Art 11(6)). The law also reaches free zones in Dubai (Art 4).

## When it applies

From the first person you sponsor. For dependants the sponsor pays unless the employer chooses to (confirmed).

## When it is due

Before the residence application, because the policy is a residence prerequisite. The policy end date sets the renewal.

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no Dubai premium.

## What happens if you miss it

Fines of AED 500 to 150,000, doubled on repeat up to 500,000, and DHA may block visa services (confirmed).

## The steps

1. Owner: buy a policy for each employee before the residence application.
2. PRO: produce the policy at residence issuance and renewal.
3. Owner: renew the policy before it ends, ahead of each visa renewal.

## Documents needed

- The insurance policy or certificate per person
