# Setting up

The day-0 list after a Dubai mainland licence is issued.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

The registrations that follow the licence: immigration card, MOHRE card, e-signature card, chamber, bank, tax, beneficial owner, and, before the first visa, insurance and WPS.

## When it applies

Every new licensee, and any existing company that skipped an item.

## When it is due

Corporate tax registration within 3 months of incorporation (confirmed). Beneficial owner declaration within 60 days (confirmed). Immigration card right after the licence; it is needed before any visa. Insurance before the first residence. Wages through WPS from the first pay day.

## What it costs

GDRFA immigration card AED 200 plus VAT to issue, AED 100 a year to renew (confirmed). MOHRE card free via the MOHRE site, up to AED 72 business-centre commission (confirmed). E-signature card about AED 120 to 300 (reported). Chamber AED 50 to 2,200 (reported).

## What happens if you miss it

AED 10,000 for late corporate tax registration (confirmed); a missing beneficial owner declaration draws a warning, then AED 15,000 (confirmed).

## The steps

1. PRO: issue the immigration establishment card at GDRFA Dubai (Amer or UAE PASS).
2. PRO: open the MOHRE company file with the licence, the immigration card and the owners MOHRE IDs.
3. Owner or PRO: obtain an e-signature card for each signatory.
4. Owner or PRO: register with Dubai Chamber (mandatory for DET commercial and industrial licences, confirmed).
5. Owner: open the bank account.
6. Accountant: register for corporate tax within 3 months.
7. Owner or PRO: file the beneficial owner declaration within 60 days.
8. Accountant: start the quarterly VAT threshold watch.
9. Owner: buy health insurance before the first residence application.
10. Owner or accountant: register for WPS before the first salary.
11. Owner: register on goAML if an activity is listed.

## Documents needed

- Licence and memorandum
- Ejari
- Owners passports and IDs

## Matching card

`setting-up` (no single compliance card in spec section 11 covers this list; the id is the library entry id)
