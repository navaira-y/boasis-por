# Audited accounts and record keeping

When an audit is required, and what the research does not say.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

Audited financial statements. The research confirms one trigger: corporate tax requires audited statements when revenue is above AED 50m or the company claims the free zone regime. Companies up to AED 3m revenue may use cash basis accounting (confirmed).

## When it applies

Whether DET requires an audit for licence renewal is not in the research: unknown.

## When it is due

Unknown for renewal. For corporate tax, with the return, 9 months after the period end.

## What it costs

Unknown.

## What happens if you miss it

Unknown. The research gives no penalty.

## The steps

1. Owner: choose the financial year end at setup.
2. Accountant: keep the books on IFRS, or cash basis up to AED 3m revenue.
3. Accountant: commission the audit when revenue is above AED 50m or the free zone regime is claimed.
4. Owner: at closing, the liquidator may not be the recent auditor (reported).

## Documents needed

- Financial statements
- The audit report where required
