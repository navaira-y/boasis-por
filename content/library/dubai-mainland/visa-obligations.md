# Visa obligations end to end

The chain from job offer to labour card, and what you must do as a sponsor.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas; mainland

## What it is

A mainland visa is a chain: MOHRE quota and job offer, work permit, GDRFA entry permit, entry or status change, insurance, medical, Emirates ID, residence, labour contract, unemployment insurance certificate, labour card. The employer applies and pays the recruitment costs (reported).

## When it applies

Every person you sponsor. Owners and investors are exempt from unemployment insurance (confirmed).

## When it is due

Residency must be completed within 60 days of entry (confirmed). The work permit runs 2 years (confirmed); the residence usually 2 years (reported). The labour contract is registered within 14 days (reported; sources differ on the trigger). The medical certificate is valid 3 months (reported).

Renewal order: passport 6 months, insurance, unemployment insurance, medical, MOHRE permit, then residence and ID. Renewing 1 to 6 months early needs ICP permission (confirmed). Residence lapses after more than 180 consecutive days abroad (confirmed).

## What it costs

Work permit AED 250 to 3,450 by company category (confirmed). Entry permit AED 200 plus VAT; status change inside the country AED 500 plus 10 plus 10 plus 15 or 50 (confirmed). Residence AED 100 plus 100 a year plus 100 (confirmed). Medical AED 250 to 350 (reported). Emirates ID AED 100 a year plus 100.

## What happens if you miss it

After the 60 days a daily AED 50 charge runs (confirmed); a work permit not renewed within 60 days of expiry costs AED 200 a month up to AED 2,000 per permit (reported).

## The steps

1. Owner or PRO: check a visa slot exists under the MOHRE quota.
2. Owner and person: e-sign the MOHRE standard job offer.
3. PRO: apply for the work permit at MOHRE with passport, photo, attested qualification for skill levels 1 to 3, licence and signed offer.
4. PRO: apply for the entry permit at GDRFA Dubai (48 hours; passport valid 6 months).
5. Person: enter, or change status inside the country.
6. Owner: put the health insurance policy in place.
7. Person, paid by the owner: medical fitness test at a DHA centre via Amer.
8. PRO: Emirates ID application and biometrics.
9. PRO: residence issuance at GDRFA within 60 days of entry.
10. PRO: register the labour contract at MOHRE within 14 days.
11. Person: subscribe to unemployment insurance; the certificate comes before the labour card (reported).

## Documents needed

- Passport valid at least 6 months
- Photo
- Attested qualification for skill levels 1 to 3
- The licence and the signed job offer
- Insurance policy and medical certificate at the residence step
