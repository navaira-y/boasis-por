# Cancelling or closing a company

The closing sequence for a Dubai mainland company, and why the decision comes early.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

Closing a mainland LLC means a notarised resolution, a liquidator, a creditor notice, cancelling every visa and card, deregistering from tax, clearances and the final account. A sole establishment or a branch needs no liquidator and a short notice (reported).

## When it applies

When you stop trading, or when you decide not to renew.

## When it is due

The whole LLC closure takes three to six months (reported). The creditor notice runs at least 30 days by law (Commercial Companies Law Art 324). The portal asks you 90 days before expiry whether you renew, cancel or shrink, and 180 days before for a mainland LLC.

VAT deregistration is due within 20 business days of ceasing to be required to register (reported). Corporate tax deregistration within 3 months of cessation (reported).

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no DET cancellation fee.

## What happens if you miss it

A lapsed licence brings monthly fines, blocked visas, a bank freeze, blacklisting after about 90 days and strike-off (reported).

## The steps

1. Owner: check the legal form; a sole establishment or a branch skips the liquidator.
2. Owner: pass a notarised resolution to dissolve and appoint a liquidator who is not the recent auditor.
3. Owner or PRO: register the company as under liquidation with DET.
4. Liquidator: publish the creditor notice in two daily newspapers, one in Arabic, for at least 30 days.
5. Accountant: settle every employee entitlement. PRO: cancel all work permits and the MOHRE card.
6. PRO: cancel residence visas, dependants first (or the AED 5,000 guarantee), then the immigration card at GDRFA.
7. Accountant: apply for VAT deregistration within 20 business days.
8. Accountant: apply for corporate tax deregistration within 3 months.
9. Owner: obtain clearances from DEWA, telecom, customs, the landlord and the bank.
10. Liquidator: prepare the final account and report once the notice has elapsed and debts are settled.
11. Bank: close the account after the final settlements.
12. Owner or PRO: obtain the cancellation from DET.

## Documents needed

- Notarised resolution and liquidator appointment
- The newspaper notices
- MOHRE and GDRFA cancellation confirmations
- Tax deregistration confirmations
- Clearance letters
- The liquidator's final account and report
