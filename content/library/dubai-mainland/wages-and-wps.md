# Wages and WPS

Salaries through the Wage Protection System, due on the 1st of the month.

Applies to: companies that sponsor visas; mainland

## What it is

MOHRE-licensed establishments pay wages through the Wage Protection System (WPS). Ministerial Resolution 340/2026 replaced Resolution 598/2022 from 1 June 2026 (confirmed). Your bank pushes a salary file (the SIF) to the Central Bank; agents pay the employees. A third-party system may produce the file (confirmed, guide s.5.4.1).

## When it applies

Every MOHRE-licensed employer with employees. Freelancers and domestic workers are outside WPS.

## When it is due

The 1st of each month for the preceding month; the old 15-day grace is gone; early payment is compliant (confirmed, Art 1). At least 85 percent of wages due must be paid on time (reported). The 30-day exemption for new hires was removed (reported).

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no bank or agent charge.

## What happens if you miss it

Reported ladder: warnings on day 2, new permits suspended on day 5, fines on day 11, all permit services suspended on day 16 for 25 or more workers in high-risk sectors.

## The steps

1. Accountant: prepare the salary file in the mandated format, one file per employer, in AED, all employees.
2. Owner or accountant: upload it through the employer's own bank before the 1st.
3. Accountant: keep the acknowledgement.
4. Owner: if payroll is delegated, tell MOHRE; the employer stays liable (reported).

## Documents needed

- The salary file (SIF)
- Bank acknowledgement
