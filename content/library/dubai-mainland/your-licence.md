# Your licence

What your DET licence allows, what it does not, and what operating outside it means.

Applies to: all licensees

## What it is

Your licence from the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) names your activities, legal form, partners, manager and address. Anyone can check a Dubai licence on the Invest in Dubai search page.

Every MOHRE work permit needs the occupation to sit within a licensed activity and the licence to be valid (Cabinet Resolution 1/2022 Art 7).

## When it applies

From the day the licence is issued until it is cancelled.

## When it is due

The licence has an expiry date. See the entry Annual licence renewal.

## What it costs

Unknown. The research gives no issue fee for a Dubai mainland licence.

## What happens if you miss it

Operating on an expired licence is reported to cost AED 5,000, and AED 10,000 after closure (no DET page found).

## The steps

1. Owner: check the licence on the Invest in Dubai search page.
2. Owner: keep every job title on a work permit within a licensed activity.
3. Owner: add an activity by amendment before working in it. See Amending a company.

## Documents needed

- The licence
- The memorandum of association
